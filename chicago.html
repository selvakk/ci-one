Chicago Attractions

Attraction Description
Millennium Park A famous park featuring the Cloud Gate sculpture and Jay Pritzker Pavilion.
Willis Tower One of the tallest buildings in the United States, offering stunning views from the Skydeck.
Art Institute of Chicago A renowned art museum that holds a collection of Impressionist and Post-Impressionist paintings.
Navy Pier A popular tourist destination with entertainment, dining, and beautiful views of Lake Michigan.
Chicago Riverwalk An urban walkway along the Chicago River with restaurants, bars, and scenic views..
